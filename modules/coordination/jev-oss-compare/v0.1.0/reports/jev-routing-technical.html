How to read this

These charts show plumbing and shape, not correctness. 69/69 means the pipeline scheduled, scored, and routed every job — it does not mean the routes were right. Probability bands are model-defined values on an uncalibrated checkpoint. The prior-run bars measure agreement with human gold labels on one fixture, not accuracy in the wild. Catch-rate and recovered task outcomes remain unmeasured; the blind replay is open work in issue #28.
